Open with the analogy, then state the technical responsibility: Bind authenticated principals to current operation/scope/route requirements and private selected contexts. Replay, cached receipts and provider selection must not bypass current scope, method, route or approval policy. Identity must come from trusted authentication. Do not treat the analogy as an additional experiment. This is a standalone talk about one responsibility, not a claim that every module is a separate service or repository.

## 08 — Failure is part of the interface

State the owner boundary before the recovery claim. Worker permission revoked before restart: Recheck at work/recovery; no unauthorized result success. Legacy worker-revocation tests. Route revoked after job completion: Retain outbox until authorized delivery can proceed. No dropped completion. Private/chain approval changed before replay: Reject altered selection or ineligible intent. Current checks and exact-result refusal tests. A fixture can position the failure while a real process is killed. Local replay semantics do not imply exactly-once physical execution, a distributed transaction, or global failover. For pure/static modules the relevant failure is selection/refusal, not invented journal recovery.
